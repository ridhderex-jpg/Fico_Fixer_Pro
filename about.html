My Story

Why FICO Fixer was created.

It started with my own credit.Gabriel's path into credit education began with his own credit. Instead of relying on guesswork, he taught himself how the system works, learning step by step while fixing his own credit.
Then it became FICO Fixer.What he learned along the way became the foundation for FICO Fixer: credit education that is simple, practical and accessible to everyone, written for people who want to understand their credit and take control of their financial future.
